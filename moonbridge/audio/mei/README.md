# Mei’s recording script · P3 Lesson 1

Record the dialogue under each heading as a separate MP3. Put the files beside this script in `moonbridge/audio/mei/` and match the filenames exactly. Record only the dialogue under each scene note.

The 23 clips cover all of Mei’s English walkie-talkie dialogue, including optional hints. The cart hint is shared by the loading, fitting, and boarding tasks. The lever hint is shared by both test runs. Each shared hint needs one recording.

## P3L1-1.mp3

*Briefing · The storm and the lookout*

Hello? Can you hear me? It’s Mei. I’m across the bridge. The storm knocked out both bridge lamps. There’s a freight cart due tonight, and its driver won’t see the crossing in the dark. Can you help me get the lights back on?

My father left a note beside the telescope. He dealt with this before. Take the footbridge on your right and see what he wrote.

## P3L1-2.mp3

*Hint · Find the telescope*

From the radio table, face the tracks. Take the footbridge on your right, beside the blue lantern, then look for the brass telescope.

## P3L1-3.mp3

*Briefing · Pick up the spare spark*

So Dad kept a spare! That explains the glowing canister back on the table. Bring it to the cream cart. It’s the only spark we have, so we’ll make sure it won’t bounce out.

## P3L1-4.mp3

*Hint · Find the spare spark*

The spark is on the right side of the same table where you found the radio.

## P3L1-5.mp3

*Briefing · Load the spark*

See the bracket at the back of the cream cart? That’s where the canister goes. Set it in there, then we’ll give the cart a short run here at the station.

## P3L1-6.mp3

*Hint · Find the cream cart*

Look for the brass bell on the cart in the middle of the station.

## P3L1-7.mp3

*Briefing · Run the first test*

Ready? Pull the red lever across from the cart. The test track has a bump just like the bridge. Watch what the canister does when the cart hits it.

## P3L1-8.mp3

*Hint · Find the red lever*

The red handle is on the left-hand workbench.

## P3L1-9.mp3

*Briefing · After the first test*

I heard that bang. Did the spark jump out of its holder? Good thing you tried it here. If it falls on the bridge, it’s gone.

Take the left footbridge to the workshop. Look in the archive for the mechanic’s note. It’ll tell you where to find the fix.

## P3L1-10.mp3

*Hint · Find the workshop archive*

From the depot, face the tracks. Take the footbridge on your left by the red lantern. The archive panel is along the back rail of the workshop.

## P3L1-11.mp3

*Briefing · Read page three*

You found a page number? Three, right. Open the handbook on the workshop table. I think that’s where they drew the clasp that keeps the canister in.

## P3L1-12.mp3

*Hint · Find the handbook*

The handbook is on the worktable in the west workshop, just beside the archive.

## P3L1-13.mp3

*Briefing · Choose a fitting*

Which way are you thinking of going? The shortcut is rough, so take the stabilizer if you want to use it. The runner is quicker on the smooth bend. Either route gets you here.

## P3L1-14.mp3

*Hint · Find the fittings shelf*

The shelf at the back of the west workshop holds the two fittings, one brass and one blue.

## P3L1-15.mp3

*Briefing · Fit the clasp*

Found a clasp? Great. Bring it back to the cream cart and fit it around the canister. Give it a tug once it’s on.

## P3L1-16.mp3

*Briefing · Run a second test*

Before you leave, let’s see if the clasp holds. The stabilizer can handle a hard run. If you fitted the runner, bring the speed up slowly and keep it steady.

## P3L1-17.mp3

*Briefing · Read the departure sign*

It stayed in the holder this time! I can see the other driver waiting at the departure rail. Go see what the sign says before you get on your cart.

You two can still race. I’ll be here waiting for the spark, whoever gets here first.

## P3L1-18.mp3

*Hint · Find the departure sign*

Follow the tracks past the parked cream cart toward the mouth of the bridge. The route sign stands just beside the track.

## P3L1-19.mp3

*Briefing · Choose a route and race*

All right, you’re ready. The track splits just past the first ridge. Take the route that suits your clasp. I’ll be waiting at the far landing, right under the tall lamp.

## P3L1-20.mp3

*Briefing · Record the delivery*

I can see you! Pull up by the tall lamp. My father used to say these lights were built to guide the soldiers who guarded the pass.

Open the book on the table and record the delivery. Then bring the spark over. I’ll open the lamp so you can put it in.

## P3L1-21.mp3

*Hint · Find the handover book*

Step off the tracks to the right. The handover book is on the low table in front of the tall lamp.

## P3L1-22.mp3

*Briefing · Relight the bridge*

I’ve got the lamp open. Bring the canister over when you’re ready. Once it’s in place, we can light both ends from here.

## P3L1-23.mp3

*Hint · Find the tall lamp*

Look right from the track. The tall brass lamp has an empty socket.
