Subtle real paper fibers and print grain, finely detailed scientific illustration but friendly and accessible to students.
Jade, blue black and warm sunlight gold palette.
Full-bleed poster scan, square to viewer, clean flat background.
No lettering, no words, no numbers, no logos, no silhouettes of people, no outside framing or physical wall.
